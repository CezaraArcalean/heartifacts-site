Heartifacts Riot — refined working font draft
============================================

Version 1.001
This refinement improves glyph isolation from the source specimen, removing small stray artifacts (such as the blob under the lowercase f) while preserving the original generated letterforms and brush detail.

Included files:
- HeartifactsRiot-Regular.ttf
- HeartifactsRiot-Regular.otf
- HeartifactsRiot-preview.png

Character coverage:
- A–Z, a–z, 0–9
- space and common punctuation/symbols
- distinctive heart-shaped A and rough x-dot treatment
